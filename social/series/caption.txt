HAI101 · Human x Artificial Intelligence
What can machines teach us about minds?

A lecture series spanning the social, behavioural, cognitive sciences, philosophy, and AI. Twelve speakers over three Fridays at LSE:
• Friday 23 October 2026 · Centre Building, The LSE Lecture Theatre (CBG.G.01) · 4 talks, 10:00–16:00
• Friday 6 November 2026 · Clement House, Hong Kong Theatre (CLM.G.02) · 5 talks, 9:15–16:00
• Friday 13 November 2026 · Cheng Kin Ku Building, room CKK 2.04 · 3 talks, 12:30–16:00

Free and in person. Each Friday has its own ticket on Luma: link in bio, or socialscience.ai.
Hosted by the Centre for Philosophy of Natural and Social Science (CPNSS), London School of Economics and Political Science, with socius labs.

Speakers: Steven Feng (Stanford University), Nick Oh (London School of Economics), Prof. Fernand Gobet (London School of Economics), Benjamin Manning (Massachusetts Institute of Technology), Helen Jin (University of Pennsylvania), Prof. Megan Peters (University College London), Maximilian Noichl (Utrecht University), Dr. Nicholas Bishop (University of Oxford), Prof. Matthia Sabatelli (University of Groningen), Dr. Jessica Thompson (University of Oxford), Dr. Akshay Jagadish (Princeton University), Gabrielle Kaili-May Liu (Kaili) (Yale University).
